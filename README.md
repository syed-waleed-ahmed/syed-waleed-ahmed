### A few things I've built
- **[Self-Correcting RAG](https://github.com/syed-waleed-ahmed/Self-Correcting-RAG)** — a RAG pipeline with a guardrail agent and an evaluator that scores its own answers and triggers a bounded self-correction loop when they're poorly grounded, instead of just hoping the retrieved chunks were relevant
- **[LLM-as-Judge](https://github.com/syed-waleed-ahmed/LLM-as-Judge)** — a framework for scoring and comparing model outputs on custom criteria, with position-bias mitigation and concurrent batch grading over CSV/JSONL
- **[Multi-Agent Workflow](https://github.com/syed-waleed-ahmed/Multi-Agent-Workflow)** — research, copywriter, art director, and manager agents that turn a one-line product brief into a full marketing campaign
- **[Multi-Agent Research Team](https://github.com/syed-waleed-ahmed/Multi-Agent-Research-Team)** — a CrewAI-based CLI where research, coding, and manager agents turn any topic into a structured Markdown report
- **[TIAGo Pick-and-Place](https://github.com/syed-waleed-ahmed/tiago-autonomous-pick-and-place)** — a mobile manipulator that maps an unknown space, recovers its pose from scratch, finds two stations by their ArUco markers, and picks and places two cubes end to end with no teleoperation
